# dev-cv.in Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 5, 2026

## 1. Service

dev-cv.in provides developer subdomains under the `dev-cv.in` domain.

The service provides DNS/subdomain registration and does not provide hosting, application development, storage, or content management.

## 2. Subdomain Registration

Users may request a subdomain such as:

`yourname.dev-cv.in`

All requests are subject to automated validation and maintainer review.

A requested subdomain is not guaranteed to be approved.

## 3. Ownership and Authorization

By submitting a request, the user confirms that they own or are authorized to use:

- The GitHub account or repository provided.
- The hosting service or deployment target provided.
- The project associated with the requested subdomain.

dev-cv.in may independently verify these details.

Providing false ownership information may result in rejection or removal of the subdomain.

## 4. One Subdomain Per Request

Each request must contain only one subdomain.

Additional subdomains require separate requests.

## 5. Acceptable Use

The dev-cv.in service must not be used for:

- Phishing or credential theft.
- Malware or malicious software.
- Fraud or scams.
- Impersonation.
- Spam or abusive automation.
- Illegal activities.
- Content designed to attack or compromise computer systems.
- Distribution of stolen or unauthorized material.
- Any activity that creates a security or legal risk for the service.

## 6. User Responsibility

The user is responsible for the website, application, repository, hosting account, and content associated with their subdomain.

dev-cv.in does not guarantee the availability, security, performance, or correctness of a user's website or application.

## 7. DNS Service

dev-cv.in provides DNS/subdomain functionality only.

Users are responsible for maintaining their hosting service and ensuring that their DNS target remains valid.

## 8. Verification

Requests may be automatically checked for:

- Subdomain availability.
- Reserved names.
- Duplicate registrations.
- Request conflicts.
- GitHub ownership.
- Hosting/deployment ownership.
- DNS configuration.
- Security and policy requirements.

Passing automated checks does not guarantee approval.

## 9. Suspension and Removal

dev-cv.in may suspend or remove a subdomain if:

- The user provided false information.
- Ownership cannot be verified.
- The subdomain violates these Terms & Conditions.
- The associated service is malicious or abusive.
- The domain creates a security risk.
- The domain is used for illegal or prohibited activity.
- The subdomain is no longer associated with the approved project.
- Removal is required for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Where appropriate, the user may be given an opportunity to correct an issue before removal.

## 10. Security Reports

Security or abuse concerns involving a `dev-cv.in` subdomain should be reported through the security contact provided by the project.

Do not attempt to exploit, damage, or disrupt another user's service.

## 11. Availability

dev-cv.in does not guarantee that the service or any registered subdomain will remain available permanently.

DNS propagation and third-party hosting services may affect availability.

The latest version published in the official dev-cv.in repository will be considered the current version.
